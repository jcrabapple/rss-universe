# Product context

## Purpose

The RSS Universe is a living directory of readers, feed tools, directories, and standards. It helps people choose tools without an algorithm, ranking, or sales funnel.

## Audience and use

Readers arrive to find a specific kind of RSS tool or browse the wider ecosystem. They need fast category navigation, concise descriptions, clear pricing labels, and direct links. The primary surface modes are Read and Operate.

## Voice

Independent, practical, direct, and opinionated about the open web. The existing editorial copy is part of the product and should not be replaced with neutral marketing language.

## Constraints

- One self-contained `index.html`; no build step or runtime dependencies.
- All runtime assets, including fonts, are embedded.
- Preserve entry content, category structure, counts, anchors, and feed generation.
- Keep dark mode, keyboard access, reduced motion, and mobile navigation working.
- Every visible link must have a real destination.
